## Ledger retirement withdrawn

The campaign Lead reverted the earlier integration (#2345) because commit `e96698218` ("local-only ordinary state") retired the
canonical shared Steward ledger for ordinary runs, which no founder ruling authorizes and which broke the in-flight canonical
batch run. This revision re-applies the R19 work without that part: `full run|record|abandon|status --ledger git` behave as before,
`--ledger auto` still prefers the shared ref when the remote answers, `scripts/steward_drive.py` defaults to `git` again, the desk
still reports a local-only ledger, and `steward-full.yml` is untouched. Competing R19 writers are prevented by the coordination
ref's slot/fence/CAS and `gate.py`'s `legacy-route-disabled`, not by changing the general run/record path.
