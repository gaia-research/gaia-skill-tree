### How to read the status words

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Source fixed** | A patch exists and is merged to the campaign branch. |
| **Independently verified** | A verifier other than the builder accepted the patch. |
| **Canonically resolved** | A fresh post-merge Steward scan lists the debt in `resolvedWork` of a receipt, and the shared ledger marks the work item `resolved`. |
| **Delivered to main** | The merge commit is reachable from protected `main`. |

A PR count, an agent run or a drop in the census is not a repair count. Only canonically resolved debts count as repairs.
