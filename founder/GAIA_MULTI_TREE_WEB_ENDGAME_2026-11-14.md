## 1. Product law

**One capability universe; many independent Tree lenses; one adaptive website.** A Tree is not a page-wide hue toggle, a second standalone product, or an alternate ranking for the same metrics. The public Skill identity remains stable while its projection, visual physics, story and supporting sections change.

Yggdrasil IV is the landing/default world. A visitor can travel to Arbor I or a future Tree without losing the selected Skill. The destination should feel like **entering another world**, while retaining the legible Gaia identity and the canonical data record. `/tree/yggdrasil` and `/tree/arbor` are proposed deep-link routes (not existing runtime claims); an optional Arbor subdomain may deep-link into the same shell rather than hosting a separate application.

This website is a public atlas of capability knowledge, **not** the Gaia App/Control Room, Skill Heaven runtime console, a benchmark issuer, or a governance authority. References: [ENDGAME.md](ENDGAME.md), [ENDGAME - SCHEMA.md](ENDGAME%20-%20SCHEMA.md), [ENDGAME - MIGRATION.md](ENDGAME%20-%20MIGRATION.md), [ARBOR-DESIGN.md](ARBOR-DESIGN.md), [GAIA_CONTROL_PLANE_DIRECTION_2026-11-08.md](GAIA_CONTROL_PLANE_DIRECTION_2026-11-08.md), [root DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

## 2. Three layers of visual authority

| Layer | Remains constant | Changes by Tree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gaia product shell** | Brand identity/Diamond Seal, global navigation, search, common Skill identity, URLs, provenance, accessibility, disclosures, contributor attribution and baseline usability | Navigation context and selected Tree indicator; not a disconnected product |
| **Tree experience** | Its contract to the same canonical Skill universe | Hero 3D shape and atmosphere, geometry, camera, material language, page accent, topology, metrics, inspectors, edge legend, visual filters, featured stories, section composition, motion |
| **Semantic materials** | Meaning of evidence Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze, Trust and challenge status, origin, verified/unverified, known/unknown; rank and star semantics stay owned by Yggdrasil | Their **placement** may adapt. Tree ambient palette may not recolor or redefine them, fabricate measurements, or turn rank into Arbor behavioral status |

The current `DESIGN.md` establishes meaningful precedent: polished evidence grades remain rectangular and material-textured; contributor Honor Red, Yggdrasil rank medallions and the gold origin wreath keep their governed semantics. Its Yggdrasil-specific World Tree brand-mark exception and Ascension visual rules **must not become an Arbor template**. Arbor green identifies the Arbor lens, not "positive", Heaven, high trust or rank. Arbor's Hell/Heaven/Ultra effects are **weather on Arbor**, not new top-level Trees and not global color themes. Ultra's gold is a transient signal, not Yggdrasil's earned precious metal.

Precedence: `META.md`/`GOVERNANCE.md` own standing and authority; `ENDGAME - SCHEMA.md` owns multi-index separation; `DESIGN.md` owns established shared/token/component mechanics; `ARBOR-DESIGN.md` owns Arbor-specific visual laws; **this document owns the adaptive website composition and its cross-Tree experience contract**. Resolve disagreement by owner, not by whichever file is newest.

## 3. Adaptive homepage, not duplicated homepages

The landing page defaults to the Yggdrasil IV world. Selecting a different Tree adapts the **full narrative program**, not merely the hero. A section may be `shared`, `tree-owned`, `replaced`, `reordered`, or `omitted` when irrelevant. New Trees are not forced to invent analogues of every Yggdrasil feature.

| Existing public surface | Yggdrasil IV | Arbor I direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero and Explorer | Sculptural gold Yggdrasil; taxonomy, prerequisites, suite/fusion, rank and trust | Independently authored green/charcoal instrument world; behavioral relations, conditions, observation coverage; **same selected Skill** |
| Hall of Heroes | Keep earned-standing/contributor ceremony and plaques | **Do not reskin** the Hall into green prestige. A discovery/interaction showcase may replace it once grounded in real results |
| Trust leaderboard | Keep TM, rank, contested standing and Origin | Do not install a bogus Arbor rank leaderboard. Feature behavioral profiles, observed interactions and bounded coverage instead |
| Scroll Ascension Overdrive | Preserve Yggdrasil's rank, branch and fusion cinematic | **Replace the highlight reel** with Arbor-specific behavioral storytelling as evidence evolves; not an Overdrive reskin |
| Evidence and challenges | Yggdrasil trust, grade, challenge/recovery, assay and methodology central | Heaven/Hell stamps, benchmark receipts, support status, conditions and recoverability prominent; underlying evidence provenance still accessible |
| Named Skill / explorer detail | Structural, rank, suite, trust and challenge lens | Behavior, interaction, conditional effect, unknowns, benchmark profile; keep canonical identity and cross-lens links |
| CLI, contribution, ecosystem, navigation, footer | Shared flows with Yggdrasil context | Reuse genuinely shared flows; contextual copy changes, not separate installation semantics |
| Meta reports | Yggdrasil governance and history | Arbor characterization and research history, sourced from real published documents |

**Arbor has no obligation to feature evidence or prestige at homepage prominence.** Its focus may evolve with actual Heaven/Hell stamps, benchmarks and behavioral discoveries. A stamp only appears as earned/observed/ratified when source data supports that statement; `not measured` stays unknown, never zero or safe.

The public homepage must not become an exhaustive dashboard. Primary emphasis is the Tree and its most meaningful current story; secondary tools remain accessible through navigation, deep links and inspectors.

## 4. Hero 3D and Blender gold-canon contract (locked)

Each Tree gets its own Blender-authored, founder-gold-stamped **reference world**. Yggdrasil has organic hierarchical silhouette, trunk/canopy and Yggdrasil-specific materials; Arbor uses a distinct angular/dendritic/interaction language even in monochrome. Do not recolor one mesh and claim a new Tree.

The 3D art pipeline is a **candidate separate repository**; separation is preferred for large Blender source, textures, renders, receipts, licensing and reproducibility, echoing `favorchurch/watershed-pathways-3d`. This document does **not create** that repository. Gaia Skill Tree retains canonical graph/projection ownership, the browser renderer, deployment, URLs and interaction logic. No new Tree data authority moves into a Blender scene.

Versioned art-to-web handoff should eventually specify:
- stamped reference images and inspection receipt, `.blend` source provenance, mesh/GLB or alternative optimized runtime assets, material/camera/coordinate conventions, loading/version boundaries;
- deterministic 3D attachment/branch anchor fields and capacity rules for existing and newly added nodes; a separate graph relationship overlay with stable IDs and provenance;
- honest canonical edge routing and non-semantic visual support geometry. A pretty branch is never proof of a prerequisite or behavioral interaction. Yggdrasil hierarchy must not force Arbor into a DAG if the behavioral graph has valid cycles;
- progressive detail/mobile budgets, occlusion and contrast, graph readability, frame/asset measurements, graceful fallback when WebGL is unavailable.

**Gold-stamped Blender output is visual canon, not a promise of browser pixel identity.** A real Three.js/web proof and independent parity review are separate acceptance steps. Do not assume Blender Cycles geometry or textures can ship unoptimized as glTF. Existing Yggdrasil canvas and art are a source of nuance to retain, not a ban on a new renderer.

## 5. Seamless scene travel

Switching Trees is a **projection and world transition**. It must:
1. preserve the canonical Skill ID and supported inspection context; move that Skill to its new legitimate projected position, including when topology differs drastically;
2. prepare the destination world and required projection before releasing the current scene where practical; use controlled camera/atmosphere/continuity rather than an obligatory mesh-to-mesh deformation;
3. transition the homepage's supporting sections and their data along with the hero, not leave Yggdrasil's rank HUD around Arbor;
4. keep route/deep-link, keyboard focus, screen-reader labels and history meaningful; honor reduced-motion and device-memory constraints;
5. disclose unloaded, absent, unknown, unconfirmed and degraded projection states without fabricating edges, scores, stamps or verified outcomes.

Quality bar: scene change feels intentional and fluid on an ordinary phone, with a truthful static/low-motion fallback. Do not require elaborate interaction to appreciate the first frame. The aesthetic is authored; the data remains real.

## 6. Two different manifests, with one binding layer

The existing founder **Projection Manifest** describes data, sources and semantic mappings. It does not own website story choreography. The website should add a separate, versioned **Website Experience Manifest** describing how an existing Tree projection is presented. Exact schema/paths below are illustrative, not ratified fields:

```yaml
tree: yggdrasil # or arbor
projectionRef: gaia.tree-projection/yggdrasil/vN
experienceVersion: 1
scene:
  canonRef: yggdrasil-gold-vN
  geometryAnchorsRef: yggdrasil-anchors-vN
themeRef: yggdrasil # ambient and motion tokens only
homepage:
  hero: yggdrasil-hero
  sections:
    - { id: hall-of-heroes, module: yggdrasil-honors }
    - { id: ascension, module: yggdrasil-overdrive }
    - { id: evidence, module: trust-evidence }
inspectorRef: yggdrasil-inspector
fallbackRef: yggdrasil-poster
```

A corresponding Arbor manifest would define **different** scene, interaction storytelling, HH stamp/benchmark and behavior modules, rather than copy the same section array with a green theme. The manifest declares what to show; a typed data adapter checks whether the corresponding authoritative records actually exist. The same Skill permalink resolves through the relevant Tree projection. The website neither computes HH/TM rankings nor declares provenance from UI state.

## 7. Architecture and repository choice

Keep `gaiaskilltree.com` as the public canonical site and `gaia-skill-tree` as its deployment/data home. The existing static HTML/CSS/JS can host Three.js and `.glb` assets. **React is neither required nor prohibited**, but no migration is authorized merely to make Tree switching possible. Prefer narrow modular extraction of section composition, Tree registry, scoped CSS, loaders, camera/scene lifecycle and URL/focus state. Evaluate React only against an explicit measured maintainability/performance requirement, with parity and rollback.

The art repo is the favored one new independently versioned boundary; the renderer may remain inside the monorepo until real cross-product reuse justifies extraction. Gaia App and Skill Heaven are separate products and must not quietly take over web/graph authority.

## 8. Future Tree onboarding protocol

Create a new Tree only after the future-Tree decision test in `ENDGAME - MIGRATION.md` is met: a distinct question, own observations/index, meaningful relations and a projection worth seeing. Then:
1. ratify the Tree's independently owned semantics and observation/index contract; identity remains shared;
2. publish generated projections and a versioned manifest, with unknowns as first-class states;
3. commission a distinct Blender world and founder Gold; validate anchors, semantic links and runtime parity;
4. define Tree-owned section story, material rules, inspect/filter vocabulary, routes and fallback through an experience manifest;
5. test Tree selection and same-Skill travel, every responsive tier, reduced motion, public SEO, accessibility, assets, monitoring and truthful provenance; founder reviews preview before any merge.

**No "one-section-per-Yggdrasil-section" checklist.** A future Efficiency or Security lens is not automatically warranted by a metric or an external scanner.
