## Decision
Proceed with the bounded single-slot coordinator and explicit operator adapter for both actions, after tightening acknowledgement-loss, evidence and accounting rules; do not invent an atomic-close requirement beyond the supplied mandate.

## Reasoning

1. **The core protocol is sound within its stated trust model.** One global active operation, atomic reservations and no post-claim lease takeover provide coordination safety—not exactly-once delivery or guaranteed recovery progress. This assumes cooperating writers, intact append-only history and a non-retrying adapter. Expiring effect leases are unsafe; a new broker would not fix GitHub’s missing conditional-close contract.

2. **The proposed blanket first-close block over-strengthens this mandate.** Existing ratified closure already uses an unconditional endpoint; coordination does not newly introduce its check-to-send race. Implement/test that existing one-shot behavior with unchanged authorization and live checks. Founder/server intervention is needed for stronger atomic-head semantics, overriding an unresolved claim, or changing an explicit authority requirement—not merely because `If-Match` is unavailable. Use:
   > “Closure permits one coordinated invocation after live authorization checks; GitHub does not atomically bind it to the checked head or holds. Ambiguous completion blocks replay.”
   
   This grants no live execution authority. A blocked closure implementation must still be reported as incomplete.

3. **Fix acknowledgement-loss semantics before coding.** Every CAS transition needs a stable ID and distinct **newly committed / already applied / unresolved** results. Reload the canonical current history after any ambiguous push; discard results computed against failed CAS snapshots. Finding `possibly-sent` is **not** a send grant. Only the original still-live call, with proven pre-network control flow and an unconsumed process-local grant, may resolve its own claim acknowledgement loss and invoke once. Restarted processes, copied tokens and recovery callers never obtain that right; otherwise quarantine.

4. **A paused claimant remains dangerous until positively resolved.** Recovery cannot cancel its potential invocation through TTL, negative GETs or local run supersession. Keep its slot and charge. An absorbing merged state can establish that another same-PR merge effect is impossible; `closed` cannot establish that for a delayed PATCH because reopening is possible. Conversely, an authentic response proving the sole invocation completed can retire the outstanding sender even if the PR subsequently reopened—record the race truthfully rather than requiring the PR to remain closed forever.

5. **Specify trusted evidence and “current” sources, not just digests.** A caller-supplied response, owner token, local JSONL record or commit hash alone cannot authorize settlement/refund. Accept adapter-captured evidence bound to the exact invocation, or independently verified forge facts sufficient for that transition. Validate against the configured remote’s current reachable history and pinned genesis/epoch. Execution authority must come from the designated current authoritative source, not merely whatever policy bytes an old checkout hashes. Cryptographic receipt infrastructure is unnecessary under the trusted-writer assumption.

## Risks

6. **Quota semantics and time remain correctness boundaries.** Preserve 2 merges/run, 2 closes/run and 10 critical writes/hour, with stable run identities and atomic accounting. Do not silently reinterpret “writes” as successful effects to justify refunding rejected sends; retain sent-attempt charges conservatively until the existing accounting contract establishes otherwise. Unknown charges never age out. Expire completed charges only when a trustworthy current lower time bound exceeds their completion upper bound by an hour. HTTPS `Date` needs explicit freshness/cache/skew assumptions; it is not automatically such a bound.

7. **Keep the first slice complete but narrow.** Required: coordinator, one-shot adapter, existing gate/CLI/runner integration, durable verifier/justification bindings, best-effort comment publication, checkout-local ordinary defaults, and truthful recovery. Preserve B/C decisions, independent verification, protections, live rereads and existing kills. Perform final checks at the adapter boundary while acknowledging the unavoidable final-check-to-send race. Keep the unattended driver non-writing.

8. **Avoid turning storage and controls into another project.** Strict schemas, permanent anti-replay protection, byte limits and guaranteed settlement headroom are necessary. The proposed exact record counts, 30-day compactor, extensive transition summaries and new shared-kill CLI are not prerequisites. Initially retain bounded full records and fail new admission before exhaustion; make repeated inconclusive recovery a no-op rather than unbounded history growth. A shared stop may be useful, but do not silently imply existing local kills affect other hosts. Preserve accepted 8 MiB/history behavior; no archive dependency.

9. **Prove the failure boundaries with focused deterministic tests.** Use independent clones and spawned processes, a disposable bare remote, fake forge and barriers. Inject acknowledgement loss at **every** CAS transition; pause immediately after claim; restart with copied identities; lose responses; present forged/stale evidence; close then reopen; race takeover, budgets and revocation. Assert actual adapter invocations **and** effects—effect counts alone can hide duplicate requests. Cover clock uncertainty, comment failures and every success-shaped runner result. Keep existing test deadlines: **no `timeout=0`, skips or weakened assertions**. Run the affected suites on the final SHA, not repeated repository-wide audits.

10. **Safe quarantine is bounded failure, not guaranteed liveness.** One unresolved operation can pin one slot/charge indefinitely; it cannot accumulate unlimited concurrent leaked reservations. Document that operational stop explicitly. Rollout must separately verify append-only/no-delete protection, canonical namespace provisioning and retirement of old writers; client CAS cannot compensate for a reset ref or bypassing clients. Source acceptance is not activation, closure execution or campaign completion.

## Confidence
**Medium** overall: the reasoning supports this design, but implementation and deployment guarantees remain unverified. Exact-SHA fault-injection results, confirmed accounting semantics and verified coordination-ref protections would raise confidence.
