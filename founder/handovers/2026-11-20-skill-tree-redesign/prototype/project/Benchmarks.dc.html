Anatomy of a receipt

A receipt pins exactly what ran: the skill's content hash, the harness and its version, the model, and a container digest. Its score is a weighted sum of four pillars. Change any input and it is a different receipt.

The six stamps

Stamps are impressions, not medallions: square, struck, and specific to the weather they describe. Heaven is split light, Hell is ink on paper, Ultra is a gold trace. None has been issued.
