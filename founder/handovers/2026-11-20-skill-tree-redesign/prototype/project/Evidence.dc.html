Per-row score

Every evidence row is scored once, from its type's base formula, capped, weighted, and decayed for age. Social signals add creator and engagement multipliers; inherited rows are discounted.

artifact_score(row) = base × cap × weight × freshness × creator_mult × engagement × inherit_mult × plateauplateau: 1.0 for the best row of a type, then 0.5, 0.25 …

Trust Magnitude

A skill's Trust Magnitude is the sum of its row scores over its own and inherited rows, deduplicated by source URL.

TM(skill) = Σ artifact_score(row) over effective_pooleffective_pool = own rows ∪ inherited rows, deduped by source

Grades and floors

The overall grade sets a ceiling on stars. Grade C allows 2★, B allows 3★, A allows 4★ and S allows up to 6★.

CTM ≥ 20up to 2★
BTM ≥ 50up to 3★
ATM ≥ 100up to 4★
STM ≥ 250up to 6★, with the gate

The S diversity gate

Magnitude alone cannot reach S. A skill also needs three distinct positive-scoring evidence types and at least one independent witness: a benchmark result, a verifier attestation or a peer review. Fusion recipes are structural metadata and score nothing.

The Apex gate

Apex, the sixth star, requires six predicates at once: five A-graded origins, one directly nested Suite, one depth-two-only origin, an overall grade of S, an A or S source held for 180 days, and a signed promotion pull request. No record meets all six today.

How a challenge reads

A verified technical challenge is struck like an assay. Severity is geometry, never colour alone. Answered marks stay on the record. These are the four states, drawn from the existing challenge components.

Open · scoped

A shallow notch: one capability under test.

Open · material

A deep notch past centre: Origin is contested.

Rebutted

Struck steel with an inlaid chevron. Permanent.

Resolved upstream

Answered by improving the skill: a plinth bar beneath.
