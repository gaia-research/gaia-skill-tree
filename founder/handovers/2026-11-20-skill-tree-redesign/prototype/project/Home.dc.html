How a skill is reviewed

Five stations, in order. Two of them are human gates. Every station leaves an event on the skill's timeline.

  1. 1

    Intake

    gaia push opens a proposal. Nothing is ranked yet.

  2. 2

    Topology review

    A curator approves where the skill sits in the tree.

  3. 3

    Evidence pipeline

    Stars, benchmarks, an adversarial audit and link checks.

  4. 4

    Ingest and appraise

    Verified rows land; Trust Magnitude is computed.

  5. 5

    Calibration

    Canon sets the rank, within the grade's ceiling.
