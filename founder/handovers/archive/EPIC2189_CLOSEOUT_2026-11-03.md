## What EPIC #2189 shipped

Yggdrasil IV is challenge-driven, reversible trust governance, ratified 2026-10-25. It changed
who may question a skill's standing and how a skill recovers. It changed no Trust Magnitude
weight, cap, threshold, grade boundary or Fusion Score rule. Every figure below is from the
fact-checked report `docs/meta/reports/2026-10-31-yggdrasil-iv-a-challenge-must-be-earned-and-standing-can-recover.html`
(source `docs/meta/2026-10-YGGDRASIL-IV.md`), which recomputes each number from the registry.

1. **Challenges.** Four real challenges ran in October. None was admitted and no skill is
   marked contested. The public record is the Trust Challenges page, `docs/trust/challenges/`.
2. **One displayed level everywhere.** The grade ceiling (S 6★, A 4★, B 3★, C 2★, ungraded 1★)
   now applies on the trust ledger and leaderboard as it already did on the named index and API.
   Frozen skills are exempt (founder ruling 2026-11-03, below).
3. **Origin by merit, automatic.** 237 families: 223 hold a classified named skill and 14 hold
   only awakened skills. 28 families share Origin. Frozen contenders compete with their locked
   score and can beat live ones.
4. **Evidence corrected.** Evidence counts once per type per source. 37 skills added without
   their own evidence now carry host-repository evidence capped at 50 per component; the first
   independent audit of that batch failed, the batch was corrected, and a fresh audit passed.
5. **Registry growth.** 75 named skills added since ratification (73 from the curator topology
   review and 2 curated earlier), 12 new generic skills plus one rename (`grill-me` to
   `interview-me`). The registry holds 784 records: 311 generic (156 basic, 155 fusion) and 473
   named (426 named, 47 awakened).

## Lessons and hazards

- **Run `gaia dev validate` before pushing.** It runs the Transparency Gate, which compares each owner
  tree with the *published* level. Changing the published level of any skill can orphan an owner-tree
  event. `build_docs.py --check` and targeted tests do not catch it.
- **`build_docs.py --check` is not read-only.** It regenerates `docs/og/`, `docs/badges/`,
  `docs/api/v1/trending/` and skill-tree date stamps. Revert those before committing.
- **`add_post.py` renders without JSON-LD.** Run `python scripts/injectJsonLd.py` after it.
- **Frozen records are immutable by design** (`preflightNamedSkillMutable`). A freeze that removes
  evidence cannot be corrected through the CLI. See #2277.
- The restored `output-skill` evidence URL is dead upstream (the skill was deleted in v2.1.1). It stays
  because a frozen record keeps its evidence; link validation may flag it.
- New `general-video` and `personal` badge and OG assets are not committed. Badges go through
  human-reviewed `infra/badge-*` PRs, and badge drift is warn-only.
